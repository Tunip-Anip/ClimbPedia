Types Of Holds

Popular Holds

Jug

A large, deep hold that's easy to grip. Wrap your whole hand around it for a secure, restful grip.

Crimp

A small, thin edge. Grip it with just your fingertips, either half-crimped (knuckles bent) or full-crimped (thumb over fingers).

Sloper

A smooth, rounded hold with no real edge. Use an open hand and rely on friction and body tension rather than grip strength.

Pinch

A hold with two opposing sides. Squeeze it between your thumb and fingers like pinching a book closed.

Pocket

A hole-shaped hold with room for one or a few fingers. Insert your fingers and pull straight down.

Edge

A thin, flat ledge. Place your fingertips or the edge of your shoe on it precisely.

Other Holds

Undercling

A hold facing downward. Grip it from underneath and pull upward, keeping your feet high to generate opposing pressure.

Volume

A large, often hollow shape bolted to the wall that changes its angle. Use its edges, top, or surface like any other hold to climb over or around it.

Mono

A pocket with room for exactly one finger. Insert a single finger and pull straight down - horrible for tendons

Crack

A split in the rock face, found outdoors rather than in gyms. Wedge your fingers, hand, or fist into it and rely on friction to hold on.

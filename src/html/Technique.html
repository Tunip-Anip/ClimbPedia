Technique

Popular Technique

Flag

Keeps you balanced with only two points of contact. Extend a leg out to the side or behind you, without touching a hold, to counterbalance your weight.

Toe Hook

Stops your body swinging off the wall, often under roofs or overhangs. Hook the top of your foot behind a hold or arête and pull inward.

Smearing

Used on faces with no real footholds. Press the ball of your foot flat against the wall and rely on friction rather than an edge.

Edging

Used on small, defined footholds. Place the edge of your shoe precisely on the hold and keep your weight directly over it.

Heel Hook

Used to pull your body into the wall or rest your arms. Hook your heel on a hold above waist height and pull like a leg curl.

Drop Knee

Used to reach further with less effort. Rotate one hip into the wall, dropping that knee while stepping on the hold with your outside foot edge.

Dyno

Used when a hold is too far to reach in control. Generate momentum with your legs and lunge for the next hold, catching it mid-air.

Deadpoint

Like a controlled dyno. Move quickly toward a hold so your body briefly goes weightless at the top, then latch it precisely.

Other Technique

Gaston

Used on holds facing sideways, opening away from you. Grip the hold and push outward, like prying open an elevator door, instead of pulling down.

Bicycle

Used on volumes or big holds to lock a foot in place. Wrap your heel around one side and toe around the other, like pedaling a bike.

Kneebar

Used to rest or free both hands. Wedge your knee against one hold while your foot presses another, locking your leg in place.

Rose Move

Used to reach a hold on your same side without crossing through. Reach across your body by rotating your torso and shoulder instead of switching feet.

Figure Four

Used on steep roofs when your feet cut loose. Hook one leg over the opposite arm, using it like a hook to extend your reach.

Campus

Climbing hand-to-hand with your feet off the wall entirely, used mainly to train power rather than in actual sends.
